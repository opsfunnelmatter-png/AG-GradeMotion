This Programme Works Best If You're This Kind of Student

Students who see the biggest results tend to share these four traits:

✓
You actually want this (not just your parents): You know what grade you need, and you're showing up because you care about getting there — not because someone told you to.
✓
You'll do the work between sessions: I assign targeted questions after every session. Students who actually attempt them before the next class improve twice as fast.
✓
You have a tablet + stylus ready: You write your working on screen and I can see every step live. No tablet = I can't catch your mistakes in real time.
✓
You know your target grade and exam date: Even if you don't have a full plan, knowing these two things means we can build one together from day one.
