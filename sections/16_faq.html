Common Questions Before You Apply

Everything you need to know before booking your diagnostic session.

What happens after I apply?

+
I'll respond within 24 hours. We schedule a Free 1-Hour Diagnostic Session to discuss your situation, goals, and outline your personalized roadmap. We will also walk through 1 question during this session to see if we're a good fit.

How are sessions structured?

+
Most students meet once or twice per week. Sessions require a minimum of 2 hours per session, completely focused on active problem-solving with real past paper questions. Structure is adjusted based on your timeline and goals.

How quickly can we start?

+
Usually within a few days of the consultation. If you have an urgent deadline, mention it in your application.

What is your scheduling availability across different time zones?

+
Since I work with students globally, session availability is strategically structured to accommodate standard after-school and weekend hours across multiple regional zones. We will lock in fixed, dedicated weekly blocks during our initial consultation.

Which courses do you cover?

+
I specialise exclusively in A-Level Further Mathematics across all five major exam boards: CIE, Edexcel IAL, Edexcel GCE, AQA, and OCR — both linear and modular tracks.

Do I need to already be a strong student to join?

+
Not at all. I've worked with students who scored 12/75 on their mock and still passed their finals with a B. What matters is your commitment to showing up and doing the work between sessions.

My exam is in 4–6 weeks. Is it too late?

+
No — this is actually when I do some of my best work. We'll go straight to intensive past paper sessions, targeting your highest-value weak areas first. Adleen went from predicted C to an A for LSE in just 4 months.

Is payment needed to book my slots?

+
No payment is needed to book your initial 1-Hour Diagnostic Session. Once we agree to work together, package fees are paid in advance to secure your dedicated weekly time slots.

What is your rescheduling policy?

+
You can reschedule any session up to 72 hours in advance. Sessions rescheduled with less than 72 hours' notice will be forfeited. For emergencies or illness within this window, official proof (such as a medical certificate) is required to allow rescheduling.
