Adleen (Singapore)

Adleen | Singapore · 2024

Adleen came to me just four months before her final exams. At the time, her predicted grades were hovering between a high C and a low B, but she urgently needed a solid 'A' to secure her admission into her dream course at the London School of Economics (LSE). We immediately structured intensive, twice-a-week sessions focusing purely on full past papers.

Our strategy relied on a 'gradual release' model. Whenever she stumbled, we paused to analyze the exact root of the mistake. I would model the solution step-by-step, and then immediately hand the reins back to her with a few similar problems to solve independently. This highly guided approach rapidly built her confidence, resulting in her achieving the A she needed for LSE.

Topic Weakness ➔ Mastery

Iman (UK)

Iman | UK · 2026

Iman sought out tutoring with a very specific goal: she was stuck on a few select topics and wanted to focus solely on mastering them. We approached this by building the concepts completely from scratch, followed by targeted topical drills.

To optimize her past paper practice, we implemented a highly effective traffic-light triage system. Green questions (can do independently) were left for her free time. Yellow questions (uncertain/stumbled) were broken down in our sessions to clarify if the issue was question wording or calculation structuring. Red questions (no idea where to start) were modeled 1-by-1 by me, followed by immediate parallel practice. This structured triage pinpointed her exact weaknesses and completely eliminated exam anxiety.

Elina (Malaysia)

Elina | Malaysia · 2022 - 2024

Elina's academic journey required a deeply empathetic, highly adaptive approach. Due to severe health challenges and undergoing rigorous chemotherapy treatments, Elina was forced to miss a vast majority of her formal, in-person college lectures. To prevent her from falling behind, we structured our online tutoring sessions to serve as her primary, comprehensive replacement for her college curriculum.

The critical element of this strategy was absolute scheduling agility. We operated on a flexible, dynamic system where sessions were rapidly coordinated and locked in on her "good days" between intensive medical treatments. This ensured that whenever she had the physical energy to learn, we maximized that window with high-yield, clear conceptual breakdowns and targeted exam preparation. Her immense resilience, combined with this highly personalized curriculum delivery, resulted in an incredible, hard-earned A grade in her final results.
