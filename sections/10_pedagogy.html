Anatomy of a Session

No two-hour session is wasted. Here's how every lesson runs — minute by minute:

00 - 05 Mins Casual Check-In Clearing mental fatigue to focus smoothly on math mode.
05 - 15 Mins Homework Review Evaluating understanding & testing memory retention from prior topics.
15 - 45 Mins Core Concept Part 1 High-focus problem solving and advanced curriculum loops.
Mid-Session 5-Min Halftime Break A quick attention reset breather before diving back in.
Post-Break Core Concept Part 2 Deep topical past paper integration drills.
Last 05 Mins Session Conclusion Lesson recap, targeted homework assignment, and live delivery of the session's annotated math notes PDF.

Knowledge Triage System

Most students waste revision time going over things they already know. We sort every question into three buckets, then attack them differently:

Green I Know
Yellow Kinda Know
Red I Don't Know

Green = do it yourself at home. Yellow = we figure out exactly where you're losing the thread. Red = I model it step-by-step, then you try the same type immediately after.
