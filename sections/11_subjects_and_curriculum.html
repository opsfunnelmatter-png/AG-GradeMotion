Subjects & Exam Boards

I teach students across all five major A-Level Further Mathematics exam boards. Each has unique curriculum weightings and assessment structures tailored to distinct academic tracks.

CIE (Cambridge International)
Linear
Compulsory Core: Paper 1: Further Pure Mathematics 1, Paper 2: Further Pure Mathematics 2, Paper 3: Further Mechanics, Paper 4: Further Probability & Statistics.
Edexcel GCE (UK Domestic)
Linear
Compulsory Core: Core Pure Mathematics 1, Core Pure Mathematics 2.

Options: Further Pure Mathematics 1, Further Pure Mathematics 2, Further Statistics 1, Further Statistics 2, Further Mechanics 1, Further Mechanics 2, Decision Mathematics 1, Decision Mathematics 2.
Students take the 2 compulsory papers plus any 2 optional papers.
Edexcel IAL
Modular
Compulsory Core: Further Pure 1 - FP1.

Options: Further Pure 2 - FP2, Further Pure 3 - FP3, Further Mechanics 1 - FM1, Further Mechanics 2 - FM2, Further Statistics 1 - FS1, Further Statistics 2 - FS2, Decision Mathematics 1 - D1.
Students must sit FP1 and a minimum of 5 other units chosen from the optional list to cash-in the 6-unit qualification (either FP2 or FP3 is required for the full A-Level).
AQA
Linear
Compulsory Core: Paper 1: Core Pure, Paper 2: Core Pure.

Options Framework: Paper 3: Product of Two Options (Compulsory exam layout, but the content inside is split into choices: Mechanics, Statistics, or Discrete Mathematics).
OCR (Standard Syllabus H245)
Linear
Compulsory Core: Core Pure Paper 1, Core Pure Paper 2.

Options: Statistics, Mechanics, Discrete Mathematics, Additional Pure Mathematics.
Students take the 2 compulsory core papers plus any 2 optional applied papers.
