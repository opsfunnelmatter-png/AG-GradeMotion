Finding the Third Root and Sketching

Use sum of roots to find the real root without tedious polynomial long division.

$$z_1 = p + iq, \quad z_2 = p - iq \implies z_1 + z_2 + z_3 = -b/a \implies z_3 \in \mathbb{R}$$
